A new look for Blitz: our brand and website redesign

If you've visited theblitzaisolutions.com before, you'll notice things look different. We've rolled out our new brand identity across the whole website, and rebuilt the site itself around it.

Why we did it

We spend our days building sales systems that are clear, considered and built to convert. Our own website needed to hold itself to the same standard. The old site did the job, but the new one does it with less noise: fewer competing elements, a calmer visual rhythm, and a more direct path from "what do you do?" to a booked call.

What changed

  • A new visual identity: our node symbol, a refined dark palette and lighter, more spacious typography.
  • A clearer homepage that walks through the six parts of the system we build, and how they come together, in one place.
  • A cleaner Services page covering each part in detail, from go-to-market strategy through to answer-engine optimization.
  • One Team page that covers who we are, how we work and the people you'll actually be working with.
  • This News & Updates section, where we'll share client announcements, success stories and practical writing on sales systems.

What's next

The website is a working example of what we sell: a system that captures attention, communicates clearly and books calls. We'll keep tuning it the same way we tune every system we build for clients, and we'll post updates here as we go.
